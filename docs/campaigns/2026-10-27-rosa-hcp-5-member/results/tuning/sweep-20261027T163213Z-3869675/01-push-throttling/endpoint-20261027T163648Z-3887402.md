## Methodology

- **P1** (local xDS push): `pilot_proxy_convergence_time` histogram delta on source istiod.
  Converged when delta `_count` >= `proxy_count`. Reports wall-clock
  time-to-converged-count plus delta-window p50/p99 of the histogram itself.
- **P2** (remote discovery): `pilot_xds_pushes{type="eds"}` counter delta on each remote
  istiod; `p2_ms` stamped at the first EDS bump. `p2_dirty=1` when the EDS bumped
  but our canary did NOT become `health_flags::healthy` on that remote's watcher within
  the window (P3 did not succeed) — i.e. the bump could not be attributed to our canary.
  Reconciled against P3's outcome, not an instantaneous health read at the bump (which
  would falsely flag the necessary P3-after-P2 apply lag).
- **P3** (remote sidecar): watcher Envoy `/clusters` polled at >= 1 Hz for a
  healthy canary endpoint. t0 is a config-only active-label flip onto a
  pre-warmed backer pod, so P3 excludes pod boot / image pull / sidecar startup.
- **Drain check:** before each iteration's baseline, every side's watcher must show no
  healthy canary endpoint (waited for up to 20s, then 5s settle), and
  again after the backer's Ready wait (the t0 check, still before the baseline scrape). A side
  that fails it is `NOT_DRAINED` for that iteration: its P3 would read about 0 and a late
  removal push would shorten P2 or P1. After an iteration whose drain timed out
  (`DRAIN_TIMEOUT`, counted: it was measured before that drain), the next one settles before
  its baseline even when the check reads clean.

## Summary

that remote's side leaves them out: `remote_status` not OK (its istiod restarted or unknown, a
P2 or P3 timeout, a canary not drained before t0, its scrape incomplete) or its baseline scrape
skew over FANOUT_MAX_SKEW_MS=1000, never the source's restart, P1 timeout or
`+Inf` overflow. A `DRAIN_TIMEOUT` side (its drain after the window timed out) counts: it was
measured before that drain. P2 also leaves out a dirty sample (`p2_dirty=1`). n is valid
samples / rows; for P1, valid samples / iterations: P1 is measured on the source once per
iteration and repeated on every remote's row, so only the source side leaves it out

never a remote that timed out or restarted.
